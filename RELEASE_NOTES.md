Chaos Hearts 2.0.0 for Minecraft Java 1.21.11: now a **datapack and resource pack**. No mods needed on the server or the client.

**Install:** put `chaoshearts-datapack.zip` in `world/datapacks/` and restart the server (it adds a dimension). Set `resource-pack` in `server.properties` to the link of `chaoshearts-resourcepack.zip` on this page and `resource-pack-sha1` to the contents of `chaoshearts-resourcepack.zip.sha1`. Then run `/function ch:admin/altar/place` where you want the trial altar.

**What's new**
- Chaos Hearts are real hearts on your health bar, each with its own icon. Every heart you hold is one more heart of max health.
- Cast with movement keys while sneaking, and shuffle between your hearts with Sneak + Back. Every ability has three variants (look level, up or down).
- An unbreakable Awakening Altar starts the trials. Each of the ten trials has an arena, three waves and a three-phase boss.
- New effects everywhere: glowing 3D shapes, screen overlays, camera shake, dynamic light and damage numbers.
- The Eye is a real model now, with a moving pupil, shroud and tendrils.

**Files**
- `chaoshearts-datapack.zip`: the datapack.
- `chaoshearts-resourcepack.zip`: the resource pack.
- `*.sha1`: checksums.

Every build runs on a real 1.21.11 server, with two bot players going through hotkeys, abilities, PvP drops, trials, the Eye and a restart.
